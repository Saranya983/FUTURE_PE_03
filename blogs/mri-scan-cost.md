# MRI Scan Cost in Bangalore

## What is an MRI Scan?

MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging) is a diagnostic imaging technique used to detect issues related to the brain, spine, joints, and internal organs.

## Average MRI Scan Cost in Bangalore

The average MRI scan cost in Bangalore usually ranges between ₹3000 and ₹8000 depending on the type of scan and diagnostic center.

| MRI Type | Estimated Cost |
|----------|----------------|
| Brain MRI | ₹4000 – ₹7000 |
| Spine MRI | ₹4500 – ₹8000 |
| Joint MRI | ₹3000 – ₹6000 |

## Factors Affecting MRI Scan Costs

- Type of MRI scan
- Contrast dye usage
- Technology and equipment
- Diagnostic center location
- Specialist consultation fees

## How to Prepare for an MRI Scan

- Wear comfortable clothing
- Remove metal objects
- Inform the doctor about implants or medical conditions
- Follow fasting instructions if required

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is MRI scanning safe?
Yes, MRI scans are generally safe because they do not use radiation.

### How long does an MRI scan take?
Most MRI scans take between 20–60 minutes.
