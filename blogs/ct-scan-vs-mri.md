# CT Scan vs MRI – What’s the Difference?

## Introduction

CT scans and MRI scans are both advanced diagnostic imaging techniques used to identify medical conditions. However, they work differently and are used for different purposes.

## What is a CT Scan?

A CT scan uses X-ray technology to create detailed images of bones, organs, and internal injuries.

## What is an MRI Scan?

MRI (Magnetic Resonance Imaging) uses magnetic fields and radio waves to produce detailed images of soft tissues and organs.

## CT Scan vs MRI Comparison

| Feature | CT Scan | MRI Scan |
|---------|----------|-----------|
| Technology | X-Ray Imaging | Magnetic Imaging |
| Best For | Bone injuries | Soft tissues |
| Radiation | Yes | No |
| Scan Time | Faster | Longer |
| Cost | Lower | Higher |

## When Do Doctors Recommend CT Scans?

CT scans are commonly recommended for:
- fractures
- internal bleeding
- emergency injuries
- chest and abdominal imaging

## When Do Doctors Recommend MRI Scans?

MRI scans are useful for:
- brain imaging
- spine problems
- joint injuries
- soft tissue analysis

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Which scan is safer?
MRI scans are generally safer because they do not use radiation.

### Which scan is faster?
CT scans are usually faster than MRI scans.

Consult experienced healthcare professionals to determine whether a CT scan or MRI scan is best for your condition.
